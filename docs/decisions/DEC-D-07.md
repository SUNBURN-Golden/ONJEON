# Decision: Decision gate D-07

Decided by Fable 5.1 (ONJEON autoloop, 2026-10-15 10:34 KST); reversible.

**Decision:** Adopt the D-07 proposal as written. Onjeon only guides claims. For each claim it shows the claim documents and claim deadline stated in the policy terms, and the insurer's official claim channel, each with a citation to the policy clause. It does not file claims on the user's behalf, transmit documents, or connect the user to insurers (no partnerships, APIs or intake relay). The guidance is the next action in the incident flow, the 'prepare insurance claim' primary button. This is a delegated decision, not an owner decision, and can be reversed.

**Why:** BLUEPRINT §2 excludes sales, brokerage and enrollment agency from the first release. Citation-backed guidance matches the product goal of letting consumers check information without the seller's explanation, whereas filing on a user's behalf drifts toward agency. A list of claim documents describes the claim procedure, not whether the insurer pays, so the screen must not read as 'with these documents it will pay'. Transmitting documents and connecting to insurers would need external transfer of personal documents and insurer partnerships, which affects ADR-0005 (PDF single import path), D-17 (external AI processing of personal PDFs) and the personal-data design. The guidance uses only public policy data and the existing screen draft. It adds no external system, money, secrets or deployment, and a documentation record can be reversed, so it is not critical.

**Options:** ['A. Guidance only: policy-stated documents and deadline plus the official claim channel, with citations. No filing, transmission or connection. (chosen)', "B. No claim guidance. Rejected: the 'prepare insurance claim' button would have nothing behind it, and the information already in the policy terms stays hard for consumers to find.", "C. Filing on the user's behalf, document transmission and insurer connection. Rejected: it needs personal documents sent out and insurer partnerships. That risks conflict with the BLUEPRINT §2 ban on sales, brokerage and enrollment agency, and it changes the D-17 and personal-data design a lot. Left to later review.", 'D. Store a document checklist or progress state inside the app, with no external transmission. Deferred: it touches the personal-input storage scope and the no-photos/no-medical-certificates principle (D-06, ADR-0005), so it belongs with D-06.']

**Revisit if:** The owner decides to include claim filing, document transmission or insurer connection in the first release or later. The BLUEPRINT §2 wording on the sales/brokerage/agency ban changes. Insurers' official claim channels (web, app, phone, mail) cannot be collected reliably from public sources, so the guidance can't be promised. D-06 or D-17 changes to allow document photos or medical certificates as input. Insurer partnerships or a standard claim API become feasible. Legal or regulatory review says claim guidance counts as solicitation or brokerage.
