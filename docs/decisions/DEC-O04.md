# Decision: Decision gate O04

Decided by Fable 5.1 (ONJEON autoloop, 2026-10-15 08:06 KST); reversible.

**Decision:** O04 동일 조건·묶음 규칙. B1 동일 조건: the comparison basis is a fixed set of condition dimensions from the label dictionary for that product group (age plus its basis 만나이/보험나이, sex, 직업·상해급수, smoking/health class, sum insured per component, 보험기간, 납입기간, 납입주기, 갱신형 여부·주기, 첫 회/계속 보험료, discounts, channel, plan type, 이율 시나리오, 절사 단위). Two observations share a basis only if every dimension is present and exactly equal after unit normalisation (원/만원) only. No age conversion, sum-insured scaling or interpolation. A missing dimension is never a wildcard. 'Not applicable' is separate from 'missing'. A condition inherited from another document counts only if the link is recorded and review-accepted. 납입주기 is part of the basis: 연납÷12 is shown only as 월평균 and never sorted or summed with 월납. The observation's effective period must cover the basis date. Stale, conflicting, condition_mismatch or not-accepted observations are excluded (K13). Users choose only from bases that actually exist, and the app never snaps to the nearest one. B2 묶음: a source_bundle price is never allocated to members and no member price is derived by subtraction or ratio. It is compared only with bundles whose component kinds and amounts match (G18), and shown in its own lane. A requires_main or requires_base_contract rider price is never added to a main-contract price unless the source discloses the combination or verified combination rules are recorded; otherwise the total is 미확인. B3 합계·차액: a total adds only accepted prices in the same basis whose items are independently purchasable or have a verified combination. Unpriced items are counted as unpriced_count / 미포함 n건 and the total is marked partial, never using 0원. Differences are only between two public prices in the same basis with the same composition, labelled 공시 조건 기준 가격 차이. No 절감액 against the user's own contract or across bases, and no lifetime totals for renewal-type products. B4: recording this lifts the O04 release gate on T-KN-09 (compare_price enabled after the I7, I8, K13 and bundle tests pass). Public exposure of the price column still waits for D-18.

**Why:** The biggest pricing risk to the information-gap goal is a confident-looking number built from mismatched conditions or a split bundle. These rules add no new principle. They make the existing contracts precise enough to test: PREMIUM_DATA_CONTRACT rules 1–7, 가격_수집_명세 §9–§10 and §15, GAP_REPORT G16–G19, BLUEPRINT I7/I8, and C-07/K13. Exact match is the only rule that needs no assumption the sources don't support. ADR-0010 keeps public price comparison in first-release scope. The decision is a reversible design record with no money, secrets or deployment involved, and the regulatory side of showing prices stays with D-18.

**Options:** ['A. Exact-match basis, bundles as opaque units, totals only for verified compositions (chosen)', 'B. Tolerance matching: nearest age, sum-insured scaling, age-basis conversion (rejected: interpolation and scaling banned by PREMIUM_DATA_CONTRACT rule 2 and 가격_수집_명세 §15-6/7)', 'C. Allocate bundle prices to members or derive rider prices by subtraction (rejected: G18 and the T-KN-09 done criteria)', 'D. Treat missing dimensions as wildcards (rejected: K13; turns 미확인 into a false same-condition claim)', 'E. Drop price comparison from first release (rejected: ADR-0010 puts it in first-release scope; O04 only gates enabling)', 'F. Wait for real price data (rejected: T-KN-09 tests need the rules now; the revisit triggers cover what real data may change)']

**Revisit if:** Real samples show dimensions the dictionary can't hold, or a basis that can't be expressed as exact equality; a verified table_derived rate formula becomes available; machine-readable combination rules (shared discounts, minimum amounts) become collectable; 보험다모아 or a regulator defines standard comparison conditions; D-18 external review requires different wording or rules; quotes_enabled is turned on; or the owner decides O04 directly or withdraws this decision.
