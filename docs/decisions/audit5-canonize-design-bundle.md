# Decision: `tasks.json:2` and `설계검토_결과/README.md:10` say the bundle is "not adopted canon", yet it drives all bot work and 09 is the completion criterion.

Decided by Claude Sonnet 5.5 xhigh (ONJEON autoloop, 2026-11-10 21:10 KST); reversible.

**Decision:** Keep ADR-0019's limited adoption. Do NOT canonize the whole 설계검토_결과 bundle through a new D-21 ADR, and do NOT stop using it as the work driver. 09_제품목적_대응표 and tasks.json remain adopted BLUEPRINT subordinate contracts: 09 is the product-completion evidence criterion, tasks.json is the daemon task source and per-task acceptance contract. daemon_rules.json remains an execution basis only, not canon. The finding conflates two different statuses, 'canon' and 'work-driver authority'. The only real defect is the over-broad shared status sentence '묶음 전체·온톨로지·daemon_rules.json·common_done_criteria는 이번 정본 채택 대상이 아니다', which can be read as covering the already-adopted 09/tasks.json. Scope it to the non-driving remainder: 01~08, ontology proposals, U-01~U-04, A-01, and common_done_criteria, while keeping daemon_rules.json explicitly excluded from canon as an execution basis only. All remaining canon proposals stay on the item-by-item D-21 new-ADR path with outside-family independent review.

**Why:** tasks.json:2 and README.md:10 do not say the work-driving bundle is wholly unadopted: they say 09/tasks.json were adopted as BLUEPRINT subordinate contracts via ADR-0019, and daemon_rules.json is an execution basis under the 2026-10-13 user decision. The remaining sentence is imprecise because 'the whole bundle' includes 09/tasks.json, which are adopted; that is what makes the finding look like a contradiction. Canon and work-driver authority are distinct levels, and ADR-0019 explicitly reserves the bundle's other canon proposals (01~08, ontology, U-01~U-04, A-01, common_done_criteria) for per-item D-21 ADRs with outside-family review. Blanket canonization is therefore both outside the existing decision and operationally unnecessary, while stopping use would overturn a recorded user decision and the completion gate. The correct fix is a scoped wording change plus a decision record and regression assertion, not a new ADR. No task, completion criterion, gate, evidence requirement, code, data, money, secret, or deployment is changed; this is reversible documentation/authority work and is not product-completion evidence.

- A (not chosen): Canonize the whole bundle through a new D-21 ADR and drop the disclaimer. - D (not chosen): Reword only, with no decision record or regression test. Rejected: the acceptance requires tests/evidence and the same confusion would recur.
